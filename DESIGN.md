## Brand & Style

The design system bridges heavy-duty industrial engineering with modern, corporate digital clarity. Serving plant managers, procurement engineers, boiler operators, and industrial EPC contractors, the aesthetic communicates absolute reliability, thermal endurance, and precise structural capability.

Drawing inspiration from refractory metallurgy, thermal power stations, and structural steelworks, the design style merges **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Brutalist / Utilitarian** structural weight. Interfaces rely on high-density information architecture, crisp technical lines, architectural grids, and distinct visual hierarchy. It avoids whimsical consumer styling in favor of robust, no-nonsense layouts, calibrated contrasts, and high-visibility status indicators that reflect extreme heat tolerance and industrial safety.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from industrial combustion chambers: deep structural slate, high-heat refractory flame, and cold steel.

- **Primary (`#0f172a` & `#1e293b`)**: Deep Industrial Navy and Slate. Anchors navigation headers, hero bands, technical data card frames, and high-emphasis typography.
- **Secondary (`#ea580c` with `#f97316` hover/glow)**: High-Heat Refractory Orange & Amber. Reserved for critical CTAs (Quote Requests, Emergency Technical Line), active tabs, status alerts, heat-rating badges, and engineering callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#0284c7`)**: Engineering Blue. Deployed for interactive hyperlinks, informational metrics, schematics, and fluid-line or steam technical diagrams.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`, `#e2e8f0`, `#64748b`)**: Crisp steel-gray backgrounds provide clean, glare-free canvas surfaces that guarantee high contrast for specification tables, engineering logs, and technical imagery.

Dark mode reverses this hierarchy into a command-center aesthetic, utilizing `#0b0f19` for canvas backing, `#1e293b` for structural cards, and high-luminance `#f97316` for heat-critical signals.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes technical legibility, geometric strength, and scannability across dense engineering documentation.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Set in weights 700 to 800 with tight tracking (-0.02em) to deliver an authoritative industrial tone reminiscent of heavy structural machinery and steel stamps.
- **Body & Technical Specs (Inter)**: Delivers micro-legibility for chemical compositions, temperature tolerance ranges (e.g., 1400°C to 1800°C), furnace brick grading, and complex contact registries.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set in Inter SemiBold/Bold, frequently styled in uppercase with +0.05em letter spacing for product codes (e.g., `WHYTHEAT-A`, `FIRE-CRETE-SUPER`, `LC-90`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column responsive grid architecture designed for structured equipment galleries, technical tabular sheets, and multi-service capability breakdowns.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) to 32px (`2rem`) gutters and outer margins up to 48px (`3rem`). Accommodates split-view layouts featuring 3D equipment diagrams alongside multi-line technical service offerings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters, reflowing multi-tier industrial services into clean 2-column or stacked card groups.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px margins. High-density data tables become horizontally scrollable cards with pinned specification keys.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a modular 4px/8px base scale. Inner component padding stays compact (`0.75rem` to `1.25rem`) to preserve a professional engineering density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors structured architectural weight over soft decorative shadows. Visual depth is established through a combination of crisp outlines, surface tiering, and micro-elevation:

1. **Surface Tiers**:
   - `Canvas`: Cool steel white/gray (`#f8fafc`).
   - `Layer 1 Surface`: Crisp card fill (`#ffffff`) bordered with `#e2e8f0` (1px solid).
   - `Layer 2 Surface (Technical Wells & Spec Pods)`: Recessed contrast gray (`#f1f5f9`) or dark carbon (`#0f172a`).
2. **Outlines & Dividers**: High-contrast, clean 1px borders define all modules, reflecting precise architectural drafting.
3. **Engineered Shadow System**:
   - `Resting Card`: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - `Interactive Hover`: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.15)` accompanied by a 1px border accent shift to flame orange (`#ea580c`).
   - `Industrial Header/Modal`: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry (4px base border-radius, max 8px on large cards and modal surfaces). 

Sharp, precise engineering requires clean rectangular forms that maximize internal data density. Rounded pill shapes are strictly forbidden for layout containers and functional inputs; subtle rounding is used only to soften tactile digital touchpoints while preserving a rigid, metallic feel. Structural chamfers or angled tabs (15° to 30° clip-paths) may be selectively used on industrial category banners and technical badges to reinforce plant safety iconography.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Industrial CTA**: Solid `#ea580c` background, `#ffffff` text, 4px corner radius, bold `label-lg` typography. On hover, transitions to `#f97316` with a subtle orange ambient flare.
- **Secondary Corporate CTA**: Solid `#0f172a` navy background, `#ffffff` text, 4px corner radius.
- **Tertiary / Outline**: 1.5px solid border in `#cbd5e1` with `#0f172a` text. On hover: `#f1f5f9` fill with `#ea580c` border transition.
- **Emergency Call / Quick Action**: Fixed bottom or header strip with high-contrast badge icon, `#0f172a` navy backdrop, and a flame orange live indicator dot.

### Industrial Badges & Material Tags
- **Temperature & Grade Badges**: Compact rectangular tags (4px radius, `label-code` uppercase) featuring a high-contrast dark fill (`#0f172a`) with orange text (`#f97316`) or subtle tinted fills (e.g., `#ffedd5` background with `#c2410c` text) for materials like `Castables Whytheat K`, `LC 90`, and `Firecrete Super`.
- **Location Tag**: Pill or soft-corner container pairing a fire-orange map pin with crisp slate text (`Ludhiana, Punjab`).

### Product & Equipment Cards
- Structural white containers bordered with 1px `#e2e8f0`.
- Upper segment features high-clarity machinery photography or 3D equipment diagrams (boilers, pipelines, refractory bricks) set against a balanced `#f1f5f9` stage.
- Lower segment houses bold technical titles, bulleted specification parameters, and a secondary action button ("Request Spec Sheet" / "Inquire").

### Technical Data Tables
- **Header**: `#0f172a` background with `#ffffff` uppercase `label-md` text.
- **Rows**: Alternating subtle zebra striping (`#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`), separated by 1px `#e2e8f0` horizontal borders.
- **Typography**: Monospaced or tabular numbers for technical dimensions, heat metrics, and material density values to ensure rapid column scanning.

### Input Fields & RFQ (Request for Quote) Forms
- Crisp `#ffffff` inputs surrounded by 1.5px `#cbd5e1` border.
- Active/Focus state switches to a 1.5px `#ea580c` border with an explicit 2px tinted focus halo (`rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.2)`).
- Clear, utilitarian field labels positioned above inputs with uppercase micro-labels for units (e.g., `CAPACITY (TPH)`, `TEMP (°C)`).
